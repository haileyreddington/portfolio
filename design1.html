"Sheets for Success" Martha Stewart Bedding Campaign

“Sheets for Success” was a partner advertising campaign I developed during my sophomore year at James Madison University. The project focused on increasing consideration of Martha Stewart bedding among non-white Gen Z men by presenting the brand as modern, relevant, and connected to comfort, confidence, and productivity.

Our campaign included a bus stop advertisement placed on a college campus and three TikTok video concepts. Each deliverable was designed to reach students through familiar platforms and environments while maintaining a consistent campaign message and visual identity.
