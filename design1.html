"Sheets for Success" Martha Stewart Bedding Campaign

"Sheets for Success" is a class ad campaign (partner) project I worked on during my sophomore year at university. Targeting non-white Genz males, my partner and I had to think of creative ways to grab their attention, so that the next time they need new bedding, the Martha Stewart brand will be considered. We accomplished this through a billboard advertisement located at a bus stop on a college campus, as well as through three different TikTok videos.
