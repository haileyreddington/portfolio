ABOUT ME

My name is Hailey Reddington and I am a SMAD (School of Media Arts and Design) Creative Advertising student at James Madison University with a minor in Communication Studies. Being from Manassas, Virginia, I wasn't surrounded by a diverse media culture as much as someone might be if living in a big city, until I came to JMU. At the beginning of freshman year, I found my passion creating media and being the online voice for brands. Fortunately, I have been able to practice this in my student organizations and internships over the past three years. Post-graduation in 2027, I hope to be living in a big city, working in media relations strategies and/or public relations!
