Armed Services YMCA Influencer Strategy for 2026

This influencer strategy presentation was developed for the Armed Services YMCA’s 165th anniversary campaign in 2026. Because ASYMCA had not previously incorporated influencers into its marketing efforts, I was responsible for researching the military and nonprofit influencer landscape and identifying creators whose audiences, values, and content aligned with the organization’s mission of supporting service members and military families.

The strategy included recommendations for potential influencer partnerships, audience and platform considerations, calculated KPI's, projected budget ranges, campaign themes, and brand activation opportunities throughout the anniversary year. The final presentation provided ASYMCA with a structured framework for introducing influencer marketing into its broader communications strategy while increasing awareness, engagement, and connection with military communities.
